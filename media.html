Visual standard

Source image before illustration

Authentic supplied photographs and verified published images are preferred whenever an ancient inscription, monument, object, or archaeological context is being presented as evidence.

Evidence image
A photograph with documented provenance or a verified published source that can support a specific historical claim.
Context image
A project photograph used to illustrate landscape, material culture, or atmosphere without claiming it is the exact object or site under discussion.
Reconstruction image
A modern graphic, diagram, or conceptual image that must remain clearly separate from ancient archaeological evidence.

Project archive

Source-image collection

These images come from the project archive. Their use in a specific historical argument depends on documented provenance and identification. A project image is not automatically evidence for every site, ruler, deity, or period.

Image integrity: cropping, resizing, exposure correction, contrast adjustment, perspective correction, and presentation grading may be used to improve visibility. Missing inscription signs, symbols, objects, or historical details must not be invented and inserted into an evidence image.
