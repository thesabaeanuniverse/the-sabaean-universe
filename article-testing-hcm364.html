Testing HCM-364: From Reconstruction to Research Program

A reconstruction becomes more useful when its claims can be separated into questions that can be measured, sourced, falsified, or revised.

Research boundary: This page states the research architecture of HCM-364 while keeping the ancient evidence and the modern reconstruction separate.
Evidence
Textual, astronomical, archaeological, or observational data.
Interpretation
Historical explanation based on those data.
Reconstruction
HCM-364 or other project-level models kept separate.

Start with the locked architecture

HCM-364 is organized as 13 Celestial Houses × 7 Celestial Gates × 4 Elements = 364 cells. Within the project, the Houses answer WHEN, the Gates answer WHAT celestial force, and the Elements answer HOW or the self-coordinate.

The four directions remain an overlay rather than an additional multiplying dimension.

Convert claims into tests

Calendar claims can be compared with dated texts and known calendrical systems. Astronomical claims can be checked against sky calculations and repeat observations. Archaeological orientation claims can be tested with survey data and horizon models. Linguistic claims can be checked against inscriptions and declared transliteration standards.

A result that conflicts with the evidence should lead to revision of the reconstruction rather than revision of the source.

Three evidence grades

[A] Attested: directly supported by inscription, text, archaeology, or repeatable astronomical observation.

[B] Defensible comparative: a structured comparison supported by evidence but not directly attested as one ancient system.

[C] HCM reconstruction: the project’s proposed synthesis, clearly labeled and open to correction.

What the 364 comparison can legitimately do

Ancient 364-day calendars show that 364 was historically used as a structured calendrical number. They provide comparative material for questions of weeks, quarters, seasonal order, and schematic celestial time.

What they cannot do: they do not independently establish 13 Houses, seven HCM Gates, four Elements, or a specific Sabaean-Agazian origin for HCM-364.
