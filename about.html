About

The Sabaean Universe

A research and media publication focused on the ancient Horn, Sabaean-related material, inscriptions, archaeology, astronomy, calendars, and HCM-364.

The site is designed to support long-form articles and multimedia work while keeping source status clear. Historical photographs and inscriptions are preserved as source material rather than replaced with decorative pseudo-script.

Editorial standard

Documented evidence is identified as evidence. Debated historical interpretation is presented as interpretation. Original models, including HCM-364, are labeled as reconstruction.

Media

THE SABAEAN UNIVERSE also publishes on YouTube and TikTok, with short-form content feeding readers and viewers toward deeper articles and documentary episodes.
