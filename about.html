About the project

THE SABAEAN UNIVERSE

An evidence-led research and media publication focused on the ancient Horn, archaeology, inscriptions, historical interpretation, ancient astronomy, calendar mechanics, and the development of HCM-364: The Horn Celestial Matrix.

Purpose

Build a source-led research archive

The project brings archaeology, inscriptions, monuments, historical texts, astronomy, and comparative research into one publication environment. The aim is not to erase uncertainty. It is to make uncertainty visible, preserve the source record, and show readers exactly where evidence ends and interpretation begins.

Core principle: ancient evidence remains primary. Modern interpretation may explain or compare that evidence, but reconstruction must never silently replace the source.
Editorial method

Evidence → Interpretation → Reconstruction

[A]

Evidence

Ancient inscriptions, archaeological contexts, monuments, objects, coins, securely dated texts, published excavation results, and repeatable astronomical observations.

[B]

Interpretation

Historical arguments, linguistic readings, comparative analysis, and scholarly explanations built from the surviving evidence.

[C]

Reconstruction

Explicitly labeled models that extend beyond direct ancient attestation, including HCM-364.

Epigraphy

Preserve the ancient text

Inscription research begins with the surviving object and source image. Transcription, restoration, transliteration, translation, and historical interpretation are treated as separate stages so readers can see what is physically preserved and what has been supplied or inferred.

Ancient astronomy

Observation can be tested

Claims about the Sun, Moon, Venus, calendar cycles, horizon positions, and seasonal astronomy can be compared with modern calculations and repeatable field observations. Historical intentionality is a separate question. An alignment that works astronomically does not automatically prove that an ancient community designed or used it for the same purpose.
