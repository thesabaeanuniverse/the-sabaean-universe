Visual Epigraphy: How This Archive Uses Ancient Stone Images

The Sabaean Universe uses authentic supplied or verified inscription photographs as source material and treats image editing as presentation, not as permission to invent missing ancient text.

Evidence
Inscriptions, archaeology, documented objects.
Interpretation
Scholarly readings and historical models.
Reconstruction
Project-level synthesis kept visibly separate.

Source image first

A high-resolution photograph should remain the archival master whenever possible. Cropping, tonal adjustment, contrast correction, and layout integration may improve readability, but those presentation edits should not alter the letter forms.

If a line is damaged or unreadable, the image should remain damaged or unreadable. Restoration belongs in an explicitly labeled scholarly transcription, not in a silently altered photograph.

Generated script is not evidence

AI-generated pseudo-Musnad may look plausible at a glance while containing invented letter forms or impossible sequences. It therefore cannot be used in an evidentiary panel or passed off as an ancient inscription.

Permanent visual rule: authentic supplied inscription → verified published inscription → no inscription. Generated imitation is not a substitute.

Building a future inscription database

The next technical stage for this website can assign each inscription a record ID and store image, provenance, script, language, transliteration, translation, dating, bibliography, confidence notes, and links to related monuments or rulers.
