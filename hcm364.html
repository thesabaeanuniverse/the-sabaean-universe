Reconstruction research

HCM-364

THE HORN CELESTIAL MATRIX is a reconstructed framework organized as 13 Celestial Houses × 7 Celestial Gates × 4 Elements = 364 cells. Its purpose is to provide a structured model that can be tested against astronomy, calendar mechanics, archaeology, inscriptions, language, and chronology.

Project emblem. It represents THE SABAEAN UNIVERSE and HCM-364 research; it is not presented as an excavated ancient symbol.

Research status

Reconstruction, not direct ancient attestation

HCM-364 brings several research questions into one architecture, but the complete 13 × 7 × 4 system is not presented as though it has already been directly recovered from a single ancient text, inscription, monument, or archaeological context.

[A] Attested
Ancient inscriptions, archaeological contexts, securely dated texts, documented calendars, objects, and repeatable astronomical observations.
[B] Defensible comparative
Structured comparisons among independent ancient sources, astronomical systems, calendars, and archaeological evidence.
[C] HCM reconstruction
The 13 Houses × 7 Gates × 4 Elements architecture and any proposed historical integration not directly established by surviving evidence.
Research rule: evidence may support, weaken, modify, or contradict HCM-364. HCM-364 must never be used to rewrite an inscription, alter archaeological data, or force an astronomical result to match the reconstruction.

The number 364

Similar number, separate historical question

Ancient 364-day schematic calendars are documented in Second Temple Jewish traditions, including material associated with 1 Enoch, Jubilees, and Qumran. Their existence establishes that 364 was historically used as a calendrical number. It does not independently establish the complete HCM-364 architecture.

Ancient calendar

52 × 7 = 364

A 364-day schematic year contains exactly fifty-two complete seven-day weeks.

Ancient calendar

4 × 91 = 364

Four 91-day quarters also produce the documented schematic total.

HCM reconstruction

13 × 7 × 4 = 364

HCM reaches the same total through three different reconstructed coordinate layers.

Shared total ≠ shared system.

Numerical similarity is a valid reason to investigate a relationship. It is not proof of common origin, historical transmission, or structural identity.

Astronomy

What can be tested directly?

Solar

Sunrise and sunset

Solar horizon positions, solstices, equinoxes, declination, and seasonal daylight can be calculated and repeatedly observed.

Lunar

Moon cycles

Lunar phases, month lengths, rising and setting behavior, and relationships with solar time can be tested independently.

Planetary

Venus and planets

Venus's morning and evening appearances and planetary positions are calculable independently of HCM interpretation.

Field method

Horizon observation

Mountain ridges, notches, cliffs, monuments, and other stable horizon features can be used to measure changing celestial positions from a fixed observation point. This creates testable astronomical data.

But: proving that a modern observer can track the Sun or Venus against a landscape feature does not prove that an ancient community intentionally used the same feature as a calendar or ritual marker.

Testing

A useful reconstruction must be able to fail

1. State the prediction.

What exactly does HCM-364 expect?

2. Define the independent test.

Astronomy? Archaeology? Inscription? Calendar? Language?

3. Record the result.

Support, contradiction, uncertainty, and alternative explanations all remain in the research record.

4. Revise when necessary.

A failed prediction changes the reconstruction rather than the source.
