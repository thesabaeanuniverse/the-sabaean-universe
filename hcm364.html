HCM-364

THE HORN CELESTIAL MATRIX is a reconstructed framework organized as 13 Celestial Houses × 7 Celestial Gates × 4 Elements = 364 cells. Its purpose is to provide a structured model that can be tested against astronomy, ancient calendar mechanics, archaeology, inscriptions, language, chronology, and observation.

Project emblem for THE SABAEAN UNIVERSE and HCM-364. It is not presented as an excavated ancient symbol.

Reconstructed system, independently testable

HCM-364 preserves a defined internal architecture, but the complete 13 × 7 × 4 matrix is not presented as though it has already been recovered intact from a single ancient inscription, manuscript, monument, or archaeological context.

[A] Attested
Ancient inscriptions, archaeological contexts, securely dated texts, documented objects, established calendar evidence, and repeatable astronomical observations.
[B] Defensible comparative
Structured comparisons among independent ancient sources, astronomical systems, calendars, terminology, and archaeological evidence.
[C] HCM reconstruction
The integrated 13 Houses × 7 Gates × 4 Elements matrix and proposed historical relationships that extend beyond direct attestation.
Research rule: evidence may support, weaken, modify, or contradict a proposed historical connection to HCM-364. The source record must never be changed to make it fit the model.

The number 364

Shared total, separate historical question

Ancient 364-day schematic calendars are documented in Second Temple Jewish traditions, including material associated with 1 Enoch, Jubilees, and Qumran. Their existence establishes that 364 was historically used as a calendrical number. It does not independently establish the complete HCM-364 architecture.

Ancient calendar

52 × 7 = 364

A 364-day schematic year contains exactly fifty-two complete seven-day weeks.

Ancient calendar

4 × 91 = 364

Four 91-day quarters also produce the schematic total.

HCM reconstruction

13 × 7 × 4 = 364

HCM-364 reaches the same total through three reconstructed coordinate dimensions.

Shared total ≠ shared system.

Numerical similarity can justify investigation. It does not establish common origin, historical transmission, or structural identity.

Astronomy

What can be tested directly?

Solar

Sunrise and sunset

Solar horizon positions, solstices, equinoxes, declination, and seasonal daylight can be calculated and repeatedly observed.

Lunar

Moon cycles

Lunar phases, month lengths, rising and setting behavior, and relationships with solar time can be tested independently.

Planetary

Venus and the visible planets

Planetary appearances and positions can be calculated independently of HCM interpretation.

Horizon observation

Turn landscape observations into data

Mountain ridges, notches, cliffs, monuments, and other stable horizon features can be used to measure changing celestial positions from a fixed observation point. This creates repeatable astronomical data.

Intentionality caution: showing that a celestial body aligns with a landscape feature does not by itself prove that an ancient community intentionally used that feature as a calendar, observatory, or ritual marker.

Testing

A useful reconstruction must be able to fail

1. State the prediction.

What exactly does HCM-364 expect?

2. Define the independent test.

Astronomy? Archaeology? Inscription? Calendar? Language? Chronology?

3. Record the result.

Support, contradiction, uncertainty, and alternative explanations all remain in the research record.

4. Revise when necessary.

A failed prediction changes the reconstruction rather than the source.
