Method

Evidence. Interpretation. Reconstruction.

These levels remain visibly separate so readers can see where direct documentation ends and modern synthesis begins.

[A]

Evidence

Inscriptions, archaeology, securely dated texts, documented artifacts, coins, monuments, and repeatable astronomical observation.

[B]

Interpretation

Historical and comparative arguments built from surviving evidence, including areas where competing explanations remain possible.

[C]

Reconstruction

Explicitly labeled models such as HCM-364 that go beyond direct attestation and remain open to testing and revision.
