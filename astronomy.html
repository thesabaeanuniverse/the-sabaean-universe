Calendar mechanics

Why 364 matters

Weeks

52 × 7

A 364-day schematic year contains exactly fifty-two seven-day weeks with no remainder.

Quarters

4 × 91

Four equal 91-day quarters produce the same 364-day total, with thirteen weeks in each quarter.

Physical year

≈ 365.24 days

The tropical year is longer than 364 days, so long-term calendar synchronization remains a separate technical question.

Observational astronomy

What can be measured directly?

Sun

Horizon movement

Sunrise and sunset positions migrate systematically across the horizon between seasonal extremes.

Moon

Repeating phases

Lunar phases and cycles are directly observable, but lunar and solar calendar cycles do not fit together exactly.

Venus

Morning and Evening Star

Venus alternates between conspicuous morning and evening visibility over an approximately 584-day synodic cycle.

Archaeoastronomy

Observation is not ancient attribution

A modern observer can demonstrate that the Sun or Venus aligns with a mountain, ridge, notch, doorway, wall, or monument.

That establishes an observable alignment.

It does not by itself establish that ancient people intentionally used the same alignment for calendrical or ritual purposes. Historical interpretation requires archaeological, chronological, textual, statistical, or other independent support.

Research standard

Make predictions before interpreting results

1. Define the claim.
State exactly what HCM-364 predicts.

2. Define the evidence.
Decide what observation, inscription, archaeological measurement, or calendar source can test it.

3. Record the result.
Preserve support, contradiction, and uncertainty.

4. Keep alternatives visible.
Test practical, chronological, statistical, and historical explanations that do not require HCM-364.

5. Revise when necessary.
Evidence changes the reconstruction. The reconstruction does not rewrite the evidence.
Evidence boundaries

Comparable
Ancient gate language, calendrical structure, horizon observation, and celestial cycles can be compared with HCM-364 without assuming historical identity.
Not yet directly attested
The complete ancient integration of 13 Houses, 7 HCM Gates, and 4 Elements into one 364-cell system.
